This Privacy Policy sets forth our data‑handling framework for WalkVibe: Gain From Every Step, our mobile step‑reward application (referred to as “the Service”). By downloading, installing, accessing or interacting with this Service, you affirm that you have reviewed, comprehended, and accept all practices laid out in this document. 1. Information We Collect

We abide by the data‑minimization principle and only gather information required to run step‑tracking functions, reward settlement, risk control and user experience optimization. The data categories are as follows:

•                      Account & Profile Information: When you sign‑in via supported third‑party platforms such as Google or Apple ID, we obtain your authorized email address, display name and public profile picture (if you choose to provide one). This dataset supports account creation, identity verification and cross‑device synchronization of your walking records and reward status.

•                      Advertising Identifier (IDFA / GAID): We collect your device‑level advertising identifier to conduct anti‑fraud risk checks, optimize advertisement delivery logic and serve context‑relevant in‑app offers. This identifier cannot independently reveal your real‑world identity.

•                      IP Address: Public IP information is captured to assess general geographic region, strengthen server‑side security defenses, block malicious access attempts and satisfy statutory record‑keeping obligations.

•                      Device Hardware Metadata: Includes device model, operating system build version, screen specifications, system language preference, time zone configuration and session timestamps. These details help us adapt display effects for various hardware configurations and resolve compatibility defects.

•                      Step‑Tracking & Usage Statistics: We record daily step metrics, goal‑setting records, reward claim status, feature interaction traces and session duration. Such aggregated usage data enables us to adjust walking challenge mechanisms and polish overall user experience.

•                      Crash and Performance Diagnostics: When the Service encounters abnormal termination, freezing or runtime exceptions, diagnostic logs will be automatically gathered. These logs help engineering teams reproduce bugs, enhance stability and eliminate performance bottlenecks.

•                      Installed App List (User Consent Required): Our integrated advertising SDK may request permission to read your local application catalogue on initial startup for improving ad relevance. You hold full authority to reject this permission pop‑up. Rejection will not interfere with core step‑counting and reward‑acquiring capabilities. Our development team cannot access or store this catalogue; all processing is undertaken by the ad SDK provider subject to their own privacy terms.

Our Service incorporates third‑party components which may collect non‑identifiable statistical information. You may consult the privacy notices released by Google Play Services and Apple Developer Services for further reference.

2. How We Utilize Collected Information

Every piece of collected information shall only be deployed for legitimate service‑related purposes listed below:

•                      Operate and sustain core Service features: complete step measurement, preserve walking records, manage challenge tasks and handle reward settlement. Your registered email is used for account validation. Where applicable, payment‑related data will be processed to complete reward disbursement procedures. Advertising identifiers are leveraged by ad partners to render advertisements and in‑app reward tasks.

•                      Two‑way user communication: We may send email notifications to acknowledge your data deletion submissions, announce material revisions to privacy clauses, or broadcast critical service announcements.

•                      Customer service support: Your registered email serves as a communication channel for us to handle your consultation tickets, reproduce reported glitches and resolve service‑related disputes.

•                      Risk mitigation and security governance: Identify fraudulent reward claims, multi‑account cheating and malicious behaviours so as to safeguard fair gameplay for all participants.

•                      Product iteration: Analyze anonymous usage trends to iterate challenge modes, optimize app performance and fix known technical defects.

•                      Aggregated, de‑identified statistical output which cannot trace back to any single end‑user may be shared for product‑analysis purposes.

4. Data Security Safeguards

We deploy industry‑recognized technical and administrative safeguards to shield your personal information:

•                      All data transmitted between client devices and our backend servers is protected by TLS encryption for data‑in‑transit security.

•                      Personal information stored on servers is encrypted at rest to mitigate unauthorized reading risks.

•                      Rigorous access permission controls are implemented: only authorized staff and contracted third‑party personnel who possess business necessity are permitted to access non‑public personal data, and they must observe confidentiality obligations.

5. Data Retention Rules

Personal information will only be retained for the period necessary to achieve collection objectives or satisfy legal retention mandates.

•                      Your identifiable account information and walking‑activity data will persist while your account remains active.

•                      Once your account‑deletion application gets approved, personally identifiable fields including your name, email address and profile image will be erased right away.

•                      To counter duplicate reward‑claim misconduct described in our Terms of Service, non‑identifiable payment‑related markers may be held temporarily for anti‑fraud auditing, and will be purged once no longer needed.

•                      Fully anonymized analytical datasets may be preserved for long‑term product improvement work.
